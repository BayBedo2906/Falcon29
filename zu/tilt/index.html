Ingabe usukulungele ukushintsha imithetho yamandla adonsela phansi?

I-TILT ngumdlalo wesimanje wokuqeqesha ingqondo ohlanganisa ukuhlanganiswa kwamabhulokhi akudala nefiziksi yamandla adonsela phansi ashukumisayo nenkululeko yamasu. Slayida amabhulokhi kugridi, shayanisa izinombolo ezifanayo ukuze uzihlanganise futhi ufinyelele amaphuzu aphezulu!
