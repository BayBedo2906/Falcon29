Game Overview & Details

The board is filling up. What is your next move?

TILT brings numbered blocks, changing gravity and chain reactions together in a tactical puzzle. Slide blocks, connect matching numbers and make room for the next move. As you reach higher blocks, the board can grow and open up new possibilities.

NINE GAME MODES

  • Classic: Plan your moves, build chains and work toward a higher score on an expanding board.
  • 4-Way Free: Move up, down, left or right to approach a match from another direction.
  • Gravity Shift: Adapt as the direction of gravity changes every five moves.
  • Rising Tide: Make room for a new row every ten moves and use the drain pump to manage the board.
  • Antimatter: Explore a different set of block interactions and tactical challenges.
  • Decay: Keep an eye on decaying blocks as you plan your next combination.
  • Block Ladder: Work through target blocks and climb the next step.
  • Super Powers: Explore special block effects and find new ways to control the board.
  • Endless Ascent: Keep pushing toward higher blocks and scores.

TOOLS FOR TOUGH BOARDS

Use the boosters available in your inventory to change your options. Undo a move, rearrange the board with Shuffle, clear space with a Bomb or remove a selected block with the Hammer. Freeze delays regular new-block spawns for a limited number of turns. Availability and effects depend on the active mode.

MAKE THE BOARD YOUR OWN

Explore themes, backgrounds and block styles. Complete daily missions, collect achievements and use in-game coins to unlock content. Wheel rewards and available spins depend on your current progress and entitlements.

PLAY YOUR WAY

Choose swipe or tilt controls on supported devices. Core puzzle play works offline. Store purchases and online services require an internet connection. Your progress and preferences are saved on your device.
