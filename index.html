About the Studio

Established in July 2026, Falcon 29 LLC is an independent commercial software and video game development company registered in the State of New Mexico, USA. We build performant, privacy-first and scalable consumer applications distributed across the Apple App Store, Google Play and Steam.

We keep teams small and release cycles short, shipping products we would want to use ourselves.

  • Privacy-first engineering

    Local storage by default; no selling of personal data.

  • Cross-platform delivery

    One codebase, native feel on iOS, Android and desktop.

  • Long-term support

    Direct email support and regular stability updates.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 1, 2026 · Last updated: July 1, 2026

1. Who we are

Falcon 29 LLC ("Falcon 29", "we", "our", "us") is a limited liability company registered in the State of New Mexico, USA, with its registered address at 1209 Mountain Road Pl NE, Albuquerque, NM 87110, USA, and is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. This policy applies to our website, mobile applications and games (together, the "Services").

2. Categories of data we process

Data you provide: the content you create inside an app (tasks, routines, save files, settings) and any information you include when you contact support, such as your email address and message. Data collected automatically: device model, operating system version, app version, language and region, crash stack traces, aggregated feature-usage counters, and — in ad-supported products — your device advertising identifier, IP address and ad interaction events (see section 5). Data from platforms: purchase validation receipts and subscription status supplied by Apple, Google or Valve. We do not process special-category data, precise geolocation, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data unless a specific feature requires it and you grant permission.

3. Local-first architecture

Our applications and games operate primarily on a local-storage architecture. Content you create is stored on your device and is not transmitted to us unless you explicitly enable a sync or cloud feature. Where local-network sync is offered, data travels directly between your own devices and does not pass through our servers. Ad-supported products additionally require a network connection to request advertisements from our advertising partners.

4. Purposes and legal bases

We process data to provide and maintain the Services (performance of a contract), to diagnose crashes and improve stability and performance (legitimate interests), to respond to support requests (legitimate interests or performance of a contract), to process and validate purchases (performance of a contract), and to comply with legal obligations. Where we rely on consent, for example for optional analytics, you may withdraw it at any time in the app settings without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

5. Advertising and identifiers

Some of our products are free and ad-supported. In those products, advertisements are delivered by third-party advertising networks whose SDKs may process your device advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), IP address, approximate region derived from the IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction events such as impressions and clicks. On iOS we present Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before any tracking identifier is used; if you decline, only contextual, non-personalised advertising is shown. In the EEA, the UK and Türkiye we request consent through a consent management platform before personalised advertising is enabled, and you can change that choice at any time in the app's privacy settings. Under the CCPA/CPRA, the use of advertising identifiers for personalised ads may qualify as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising; California residents can opt out using the "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" control in the app settings or by emailing us. We do not sell personal information for money. Where a product offers an ad-free purchase or subscription, advertising is disabled once the purchase is validated.

6. Third-party processors

We rely on a limited set of providers acting on our behalf or as independent controllers: Apple (App Store, In-App Purchase, Game Center, CloudKit), Google (Google Play, Play Billing, Play Integrity), Valve (Steam), advertising and monetisation partners such as Google AdMob or Ad Manager, Unity Ads and AppLovin, and crash-reporting and hosting providers. Advertising partners act as independent controllers for the data collected through their SDKs, and their own privacy policies govern that processing. We enter into data-processing agreements with our processors where required by law.

7. International transfers

Our operations and some providers are located in the United States. Where personal data of users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or Türkiye is transferred outside those jurisdictions, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent lawful transfer mechanism, together with supplementary technical measures such as encryption in transit.

8. Retention

Support correspondence is retained for up to 24 months after the request is closed. Diagnostic and crash data is retained for up to 12 months in aggregated or anonymised form. Purchase records are retained for the period required by tax and accounting law. Locally stored content is retained on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CCPA/CPRA and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK No. 6698), you may request access to your personal data, correction of inaccurate data, erasure, restriction of or objection to processing, portability in a machine-readable format, and withdrawal of consent. Requests can be sent to contact@falcon29.com and are answered within 30 days, free of charge. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

10. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures, including TLS encryption in transit, access controls on administrative systems, and minimisation of the data we collect. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. In the event of a personal data breach likely to result in a risk to your rights, we will notify the competent authority and, where required, affected users without undue delay.

11. Children

Our products are not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Where an app is listed in a children's category or is directed to mixed audiences, we disable personalised advertising and instruct our advertising partners to serve only non-personalised, age-appropriate ads, in line with COPPA, the GDPR and store policies. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

12. Cookies and website analytics

This website uses only strictly necessary storage required to remember your language preference. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies on this site.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our products or in applicable law. Material changes will be published on this page with a new effective date and, where required, announced in-app before taking effect.

14. Contact

Privacy questions and rights requests: contact@falcon29.com. Postal correspondence can be requested by email.
