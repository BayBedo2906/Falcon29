Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Falcon 29 LLC ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting user privacy. This policy governs how our applications and games handle data.

1. Data Collection & Analytics: Our games and apps operate primarily on local storage architecture. We do not collect or sell personal information without explicit informed consent. Anonymized crash logs or telemetry may be collected strictly to maintain application stability.

2. Third-Party Services: Distribution and platform features (such as Apple Game Center, In-App Purchases, or CloudKit) are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

3. Contact for Inquiries: If you have questions regarding our data practices, contact us at contact@falcon29.com.
